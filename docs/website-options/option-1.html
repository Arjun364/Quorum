How Quorum handles your minutes

  • Separate by company

    Each company sees only its own organizations, departments and minutes.

  • Links, not minutes

    Emails say that minutes are ready and link into the app. They never contain the minutes.

  • Hashed passwords

    Passwords are stored hashed. A reused session token ends every session of that person.

  • A history that stays

    Every version of a point is kept, with who changed it and when.

Questions

What people ask before they sign up.

Contact the team

Who writes the minutes?

One person present, drawn at random for each meeting. The head starts the meeting and approves it, but never writes.

What if an owner does not answer?

A reminder goes out after 2 working days and the point is flagged after 4. Your department can change both.

Is Quorum a task manager?

No. Daily tasks stay where you keep them. Quorum records what was decided and the state of each point at every meeting.

Can we use our own logo and address?

Yes. The company head sets the name, logo and colours, and the company gets its own address.

Do emails contain the minutes?

No. They name the meeting and link into the app, where people confirm.
