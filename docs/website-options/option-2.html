Questions

Plain answers to the usual questions.

Contact the team

How many people start a meeting?

At least three from the department, the head included. Guests from other departments can join but are never drawn.

Who sees the minutes?

People in the department see them after the note-taker submits. Before that, only the head and the note-taker.

Can a point be disputed?

After three comment rounds a point is marked disputed and shown to the head in review.

Is there a price?

Price on request.

Does it replace Zoho Projects?

No. Daily tasks stay in your task tool. Quorum keeps the meeting record.
