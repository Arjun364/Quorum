Questions

Before you start your meeting.

Contact the team

Do I write the minutes?

No. A person present is drawn for each meeting. You start it, add your own notes and approve.

What if the draw picks someone absent?

It cannot. Only people marked present are drawn.

Can I move my own note-taker?

Yes, with a reason that is written down.

Where do my people sign in?

At your company’s own address.

Does Quorum assign tasks?

No. It records points and their state at each meeting.
